Our Eco Commitment

"Eco House" is our promise. We're committed to sustainable tourism that respects our environment and supports our local community, ensuring your stay is both comfortable and conscious.

Eco-Construction

Buildings made from local wood, bamboo, and recycled materials, designed for natural airflow.

Water Conservation

100% greywater treatment for garden irrigation and low-flow fixtures throughout.

Renewable Energy

Solar water heating, LED lighting, and energy-efficient appliances minimize our footprint.

Key Practices

Zero Single-Use Plastic Recycling & Composting Locally Sourced Food Hiring Local Staff
