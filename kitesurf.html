Ride the Wind

Mancora is a world-renowned kitesurfing spot, thanks to our consistent thermal winds and warm waters. Come see why kiters return here year after year, right from our doorstep.

IKO Certified Lessons

Learn or progress safely with our partner IKO certified school, Wild Kitesurf Peru, operating right here. We ensure top standards with:

  • Professional, certified instructors
  • Latest Duotone equipment
  • Radio helmets for clear communication
  • Safety boat support

Courses available for all levels, from your first Discovery flight to advanced coaching.

Lessons available as add-ons when booking your stay.

Beyond the Basics: Wave Riding

Ready to take your riding into the waves? Mancora offers fantastic conditions for learning and progressing in wave kitesurfing.

  • Specialized Coaching: Focused sessions on wave technique, transitions, and strapless riding.
  • Kitesurf Wave Camps: Immerse yourself in a multi-day camp hosted right here at Eco House, designed for dedicated wave skill progression with video analysis and expert guidance.

Wave coaching and camps can be booked in advance or upon arrival.

Explore Peru's Coast

Discover more of Peru's legendary coastline with guided kitesurf adventures organized by Wild Kitesurf Peru.

  • Downwinders: Experience the thrill of riding downwind along stunning stretches of coast with vehicle support.
  • Kite Trips: Guided excursions to famous nearby wave spots like Lobitos or Cabo Blanco, tailored to conditions and your level.
  • Local Expertise: Benefit from the knowledge of local guides who know the conditions and spots inside out.

Inquire about trips and downwinders when booking or during your stay.

The Spot: Conditions

The Wind

Consistent side-onshore thermal winds. Best season is April to December. Average 18-25 knots, perfect for 7m-10m kites.

The Water

Warm water all year - no wetsuit needed! A mix of flat water inside the reef and fun waves further out. Wide, sandy beach for setup.

Your Kiting Hub: Gear & Storage

Travel light! We offer high-quality Duotone & Core kites/boards for rent directly on-site. Secure storage is also available for your own gear. All rentals and storage can be added when booking your stay.
