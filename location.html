How to Get Here

Reaching Eco House Mancora is straightforward. We recommend flying into one of the nearby airports and taking a taxi or arranging a transfer with us.

By Air:

Fly into Talara (TAL ~1h10 taxi), Tumbes (TBP ~1h45 taxi), or Piura (PIU ~2h30 taxi). All receive daily flights from Lima (LIM).

By Bus:

Comfortable overnight buses run daily from Lima, Guayaquil (Ecuador), and other major cities directly to Mancora.
