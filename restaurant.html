Nourish Your Body

Our on-site restaurant and bar, "Nourish," is the heart of our hotel. We believe that good food is a key part of a good life, blending fresh Peruvian ingredients with healthy international flavors.

Hyper-Local

Sourced daily from nearby farms and fishermen for peak freshness.

Authentic Peruvian

Celebrating vibrant local cuisine like fresh ceviche and lomo saltado.

Healthy & Vibrant

Fuel your adventures with poke bowls, fresh juices, and smoothie bowls.

Ocean View Dining

Enjoy delicious meals and stunning sunsets with panoramic Pacific views.

Local, Fresh, Sustainable

Our menu is a celebration of Peru's incredible bounty. We work directly with local farmers and fishermen to source the freshest ingredients daily, creating dishes that are both delicious and responsible.

Breakfast

Smoothie bowls, avocado toast, Peruvian coffee, and fresh-squeezed juices. Included with your stay!

Lunch & Dinner

Fresh ceviche, fish tacos, quinoa salads, lomo saltado, wood-fired pizzas, and daily specials.

The Bar

Pisco sours, chilcanos, craft beers, wine selection, and healthy non-alcoholic elixirs.

Signature Dishes

Classic Ceviche

Fresh catch of the day marinated in lime juice, spiced with aji limo chili, red onions, and cilantro.

Lomo Saltado

Traditional Peruvian stir-fry with tender beef strips, onions, tomatoes, soy sauce, and french fries.

Andean Quinoa Bowl

Nutrient-packed bowl with organic quinoa, avocado, seasonal roasted vegetables, and a passion fruit vinaigrette.

Baja Fish Tacos

Crispy battered fish, shredded cabbage, pico de gallo, and chipotle crema on warm corn tortillas.
