Catch the Perfect Wave

Mancora offers legendary waves right on our doorstep and access to the diverse breaks of Peru's North Shore. From beginner lessons to immersive surf camps and guided trips, we have your surfing adventure covered.

The Surf Conditions.

Water Temperature

Enjoy warm waters year-round (approx. 20-25°C / 68-77°F). Boardshorts or a light top are all you need!

Swell Conditions

The Peruvian Northshore receives both north and south swells, allowing a huge variety of spots and conditions year-round.

World-Class Spots Nearby

Here is a non-exhaustive list of spots where you may ride: El Ñuro, El Golf, el Muelle, baterías, Negritos, Punta Balcones, Lobitos, Piscinas, Peña redonda, La Cruz, Caleta Grau, La Frontera, Cabo Blanco, Panic Point. Etc.

Lessons & Coaching for All Levels

Learn or improve with our experienced local instructors, focusing on safety, fun, and progression right here in Mancora.

  • Beginner Lessons: Master the basics of paddling, pop-ups, and wave etiquette.
  • Intermediate Coaching: Improve technique, wave reading, and start carving turns.
  • Pro Coaching: Take your skills to the next level with advanced techniques and personalized video analysis.

Lessons available as add-ons when booking your stay.

Discover More Waves: Guided Surf Trips

Explore the legendary Peruvian North Shore beyond Mancora. Join our guided daily trips to discover a variety of surf breaks.

  • Access diverse spots like Lobitos, Cabo Blanco, El Nuro, and more.
  • Benefit from local expert guides who find the best waves based on daily conditions.
  • Comfortable transport and support included.

Inquire about daily surf trips when booking or upon arrival.

The Mancora Surf Lifestyle

Wake up, check the waves from your room, and surf the point break just steps away. Refuel with a healthy breakfast, join a surf trip or yoga session, or simply relax by the pool. In the evening, enjoy fresh local food at Nourish or explore the vibrant restaurants and Pisco Sour bars in Mancora town. Repeat!
