Our Sacred Space

Practice with the sound of the ocean and a gentle breeze. Our shala is built from local, eco-friendly materials and offers a panoramic view of the coastline. It's the perfect place to start your day or unwind at sunset.

  • Open-air with ocean views
  • Natural, sustainable materials
  • Mats, blocks, and props provided
  • Open for guest self-practice

Classes & Styles

We offer 2-3 classes daily, led by our certified resident and guest instructors. Private classes are also available upon request.

  • Morning Vinyasa: An energetic flow to awaken your body and set your intention for the day.
  • Sunset Yin/Restorative: A slow, deep-stretch class to restore your muscles after a long day of surfing or kiting.
  • SUP Yoga: Take your practice to the water! A fun challenge for your balance and core (seasonal).

Class schedule available upon check-in. Can be added to your stay.

The Eco House Yoga Experience

Begin your day with an energizing morning flow as the sun rises over the Pacific. After a day of adventure, return to the shala for a restorative sunset session, stretching your muscles and calming your mind to the sound of the waves.
